        Drive it yourself

        Climb into a tank and fight from the driver's seat. The other tanks drive themselves.

        W S drive forward and back · A D aim (the hull follows your gun) · Space fire. Arrow keys work too. The gap in the crosshair is your shot spread; the ring fills as the gun reloads; the radar ring is your gun range.

          Balance: every stat point is tuned to be worth about the same, checked by running thousands of duels between random builds. No single slider wins on its own; the mix and the matchups decide. Player names have no password, so only edit your own tanks.
