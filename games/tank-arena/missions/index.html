TANK ARENA

Pick a mode.

    Keyboard and mouse

    W A S D to drive. Mouse aims, click fires. Space or right-click drops a mine. P pauses.

    Keyboard only

    W A S D to drive. Arrow keys aim that way and fire (hold two for diagonals). F fires where the turret points. Space drops a mine.

    Phone

    The page turns into a handheld console. The joystick drives, Fire shoots at the nearest enemy (hold it down), Mine drops a mine. You can also tap the screen to shoot at that spot.

    Rules

    One hit destroys any tank, including yours. Shells bounce once off walls. Mines blow after a few seconds, or when a tank gets close, and they break orange blocks. Shells fly over pits; tanks can't cross them.

    Survival scoring

    Tougher tanks are worth more. Kills in quick succession build a multiplier up to ×5. Clearing a wave pays a bonus. Three lives. Scores are kept on this device.
