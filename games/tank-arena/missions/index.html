Keyboard and mouse

W A S D or arrows to drive. Mouse aims, click fires. Space or right-click drops a mine. P pauses.

Touch

Drag in the lower-left of the arena to drive. Tap anywhere else to aim and fire there. Use the mine button underneath.

Rules

One hit destroys any tank, including yours. Shells bounce once off walls. Mines blow after a few seconds, or when a tank gets close, and they break orange blocks. Shells fly over pits; tanks can't cross them.
