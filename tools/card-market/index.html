Pokémon Card Market

What cards sell for on TCGplayer and Cardmarket, which ones are climbing or sliding, and how each price has moved over time.

Prices come from TCGplayer (US dollars) and Cardmarket (euros) through the open TCGdex API. The market overview is a daily snapshot of the newest sets; search covers every card. For information only, not buying or selling advice. This is an unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.
